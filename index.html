What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data from digital threats. It involves using various tools and techniques to prevent unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification or destruction of information. As more aspects of our lives move online, cybersecurity becomes increasingly important to safeguard personal and business information.
